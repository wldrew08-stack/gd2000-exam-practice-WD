## 1. Is it a game?

The course defines a game as "a voluntary, structured play activity that
players do for fun." Pick one of Cookie Clicker, The Stanley Parable, or
Minecraft in Creative Mode. Argue whether it is a game under that definition,
and say which part of the definition it stresses or breaks.

Cookie Clicker. The game does have a structure to its play in objectives and mechanisms.
Your objective is to grow your cookie production to biblical levels by reinvesting cookies into production.
You can only increases production by buying more grandmas, farms, mines etc. buying upgrades 
for those production modes or just clicking the big cookie and making more yourself.
The game does stress the definition because there is no end and you don't have any threat factor 
that may prevent you from achieving your production. However I find it to still be a game.

## 3. Chess and Texas Hold'em

Both are two-player competitive games. Describe how hidden information and
randomness change what the player is actually doing in each. Then give one
concrete design consequence: something you would have to change if you moved
chess's rules to poker's information model, or the other way around.

In Chess, all pieces are seen, all their possible movements this turn are visible and future moves are predictable.
Poker has every players hand completely hidden, the table hand hidden, the future hidden and possible combinations hidden.
Chess incentives prediction and strategy to out think your enemy. -out planning
Poker incentives prediction and bluffing to out gamble your enemy. -out risking

If chess were to have a degree of limited information, that would cause blind spots 
to for in players planning, predictions and knowing future moves. This would cause 
people to become more cautious in their moves and reward risky surprises rather than raw numbers.

## 4. Tetris

In class almost everyone got bored before playing a full hour of Tetris. Why?
What would you change about the game to keep a player interested for that
long, and what would that change cost?

The problem with continuously playing Tetris is it's very simple and endless.
The loop is very fast with just place block, clear row, place block, clear row.
Even just adding a little more things happening like power ups that change the game a little 
could make you pay attention more and change the loop for a time, resetting your boredom.
This would cost some of the simplicity and reliability of the loop but it may be worth the added attention.

## 5. The game loop

Pick one of: Go, Blackjack, Settlers of Catan, Pandemic, Slay the Spire,
Hades, Minecraft, Stardew Valley, Overcooked, Pac-Man.

Describe its game loop: what the player does, what the game updates in
response, and what feedback the player gets. Then remove one step of the loop
and describe what breaks.

Game:Catan

Each player is trying to form a civilization worth 10 points in as few turns as possible. 
Each player roles dice to gain a certain amount of resources from their settlements. 
They trade those resources for other resources (Players/game) or exchange those resources 
for roads, cities, towns and development cards. All these tools can be used for achieving the 10 points toward victory.

If I remove the trading between just players, then the game crawls to a halt and may be stopped indeterminately 
because some players can not reach required resources and so every player struggles to leap over one hurdle.
